# Art Direction References

## Visual North Star
**mariavareva.com** — serif-led, clean, not overwhelming, Apple-style scroll transitions.
This is the closest match to Elaine's target aesthetic. Elegant, typographic, minimal but impactful.

## Design Principles Derived

- **Color discipline**: Teal is interactions-only (hover states, active CTAs, links). Not decorative.
- **Motion**: Scroll-triggered reveals (opacity + translateY), Apple-style easing. Entrance animation on hero load.
- **Personality**: Woven into copy and one distinct section — not illustration.
- **AI positioning**: Elaine's AI work should be prominent in framing, not just one card among many.
- **Case study cards**: Impact metrics visible early (not buried at end).
- **Performance**: Keep it lean. No frameworks, minimal JS, no heavy animations.
